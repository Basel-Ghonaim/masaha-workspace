# The owner's autonomy rule — 2026-10-25, while he is away (≈ 1 hour)

**From the owner, through the main supervisor:** keep working; do not stop to ask. Where your prompt says
"stop and ask" for a **decision** (a choice the plan or prompt leaves open, a plan that proves false against the
code, a departure, a break that stays green, a carried item larger than its file says, a file count past the
limit, a real-run case the demo data cannot hold), **decide it yourself**: take the option that best keeps the
plan's intent, the documents' rules and v1 scope, the smaller and safer change first. Then carry on.

**Record every such decision** in your report as you go, and in the final reply under a heading
`Decisions taken without the owner`: each with what you met, the options, what you chose and why, and how to
undo it. The owner reviews them on his return; any may be reversed.

**Still never, whatever you decide** (these are not decisions, they are limits):
- merge, push to `main`, force-push another branch, or create an Issue; delete a remote branch;
- build anything on the *Not in v1* list;
- reset, reseed, drop or change data of a database through the app to pass a case (record the case as not
  run, with why, and continue); touch another worker's folder, database or ports;
- skip, `.skip` or delete a test, or weaken a config, timeout, lane or check to make it pass;
- print a secret, a token or a demo phone number; add tool attribution.

**A command you cannot run:** find the root cause (up to three tries); if it stays blocked, record it under
COULD NOT RUN with the exact error, continue with every step it does not block, and let CI judge on the pushed
head. Only when nothing at all can continue, write where the work stands in the report and end.
