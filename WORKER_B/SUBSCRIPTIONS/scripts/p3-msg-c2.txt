feat(desk): check a subscriber in

The desk checks a subscriber in on one of their subscriptions (the
owner's decision 9), in one transaction under the space's lock: the
key's check-in for a retry; the subscription, the space's; its
customer neither archived (CUSTOMER_ARCHIVED) nor present by a
check-in or a visit (MEMBER_ALREADY_CHECKED_IN); then the warnings,
never a refusal: the space full, outside its hours, and four new
ones from the subscription - not started, expired, this week's days
used up (only for a new day), today's hours used up.

The limits are a pure rule (limits.ts) on subscription_usage's
counts. A retry's limits leave the check-in itself out, so a retry
answers the warnings its first request did (S7). A key that checked
in another subscription is a conflict, as a payment's and a
subscribe's are.
