feat(desk): receive a subscription's payment

The desk's payment route now pays exactly one item: a closed visit, as
before, or a subscription of the space, whose row it locks after the
space's. A subscription takes a payment whatever its status, even an
archived customer's: its debt is still owed. The ledger refuses an
amount above a fixed price; a usage-based subscription takes any, and
answers its credit as a balance below zero.

A key that paid another item is a conflict either way. The ledger's
record names its item, so one record and one audit entry serve both.
The answer gains the subscription, and its visit becomes nullable.
