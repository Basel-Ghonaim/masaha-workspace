fix(desk): close a subscriber's stay at its due

A subscriber's check-out closed the check-in at now, while a visit's
check-out stops at the stay's due time and the data model says no path
charges a stay into the next day. An overnight check-in closed the next
morning counted every hour to then in the subscription's minutes and
due, which no route corrects.

The check-out now closes at the earlier of now and the due time, from
the space's week and settings read under its lock. The cap is one
helper of spaces, stayEnd, which the visit's check-out now uses too, so
the two stays share one rule. The audit keeps the request's own time.
