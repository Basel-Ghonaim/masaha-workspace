feat(subscriptions): end a subscription early

The staff end a subscription early: its end becomes today, or its
start if it has not started, never later than its end was, with who
ended it and when, audited. It is expired from then, and a repeat
answers it as it is.

Ending changes no money: what is owed stays owed, with no refund or
proration, and an open check-in on it stays open (decision 5). The
end shares the corrections' opening: the space's lock, can() under it
and the subscription's row.
