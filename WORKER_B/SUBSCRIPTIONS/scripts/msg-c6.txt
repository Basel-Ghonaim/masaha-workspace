feat(subscriptions): correct a subscription

The staff correct a subscription's limits, dates and name; only the
owner corrects its price and billing, on a route of its own refused
to reception before the body is read, with a reason recorded as the
owner's typed price. Each correction is audited with what changed, and
one that changes nothing writes nothing; an ended subscription is no
longer corrected (SUBSCRIPTION_ENDED).

A fixed price below what was paid is the database's refusal, through
the price trigger, now translated to PRICE_BELOW_PAID. The name is
correctable as the owner chose (Q8, A3).
