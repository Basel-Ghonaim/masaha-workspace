feat(desk): read a subscription's statement

The desk answers one subscription's statement: its state, what was paid
and what it owes, its customer, every check-in with a closed stay's
whole minutes (an open one's are null), and every payment, voided ones
marked. While no subscription renews it, it proposes the start a
renewal would take, by the same rule the subscribe uses; once renewed,
it names the renewal instead.

Its lists are whole: they are bounded by one subscription, an
exception the contract's §4 now lists.
