feat(desk): check a subscriber out

The desk closes a subscriber's check-in now, MANUAL, audited
checkIn.checkedOut, under the space's lock and then the check-in's
row. The answer carries what was paid on the subscription and what
it still owes, so a usage-based subscriber's desk sees what the stay
cost; the payment stays POST .../payments' (alternative A5 not
taken). A check-in closed already, by the desk or the auto
check-out, is answered as it is: closing is idempotent by design,
so the route takes no key.
