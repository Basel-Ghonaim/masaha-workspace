feat(subscriptions): list check-ins

The staff read the space's subscriber check-ins two ways: open=true,
every open one, the earliest first, whole (bounded by who is
present, an exception of api-contract §4); or a range of Gaza dates
of at most 92 days, paged, the latest first. The range is dated in
SQL as subscription_usage dates attendance, so a check-in after
midnight in Gaza falls on its Gaza day, whatever its UTC date.

Each answer carries its customer and its subscription's state, two
queries for the lot (deskCheckIns), which the desk's check-in and
check-out now answer through too. Step 8's API is complete.
