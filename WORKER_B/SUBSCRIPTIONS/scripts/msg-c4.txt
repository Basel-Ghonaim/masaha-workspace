feat(desk): renew, paying the oldest debt first

A subscribe may now renew one of the customer's subscriptions, at most
once, and start, unless the desk says otherwise, the day after the
renewed one's end while it runs. It may also carry an amount: the
server allocates it over the customer's items that still owe, oldest
first, each at most its balance, and gives the rest to the new
subscription, one payment per item, all in the request's transaction.
The ledger still refuses a rest above a fixed new price, and then
nothing is written.

Each payment's key is derived from the request's and its place, so a
retry finds the subscription and its payments without a new column on
the append-only ledger. The desk is warned (CUSTOMER_HAS_DEBT) while an
older debt remains; a second renewal is SUBSCRIPTION_ALREADY_RENEWED.
