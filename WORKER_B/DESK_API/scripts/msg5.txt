feat(desk): read a statement from one snapshot

A statement's paid and balance, its payments and its check-ins were
separate queries on the pool: a payment or a check-in committed between
them answered sums that its own lists contradict (finding 124). The
statement and the customer's file now run their reads in one read-only
REPEATABLE READ transaction, a new injected port beside runInTransaction,
passing its tx to every read; three reads gain the optional tx.

The race tests hold the visits' table, which only the money read touches,
while a check-in and a payment commit; under READ COMMITTED they fail.
