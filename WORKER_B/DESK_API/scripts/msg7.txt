feat(auth): end reception's session when idle

The owner's revision of 2026-10-24: a shared desk left open must not stay
signed in. A session of an account with an active RECEPTION link, no
OWNER link and not the admin now ends after 15 minutes with no request.

The family carries its last use (one column, set at sign-in, copied on
rotation). requireAuth reads the account's link roles, then for reception
the session: idle past 15 minutes it answers 401 SESSION_IDLE and keeps
the family; otherwise it renews the last use, at most once a minute,
unless the route is passive - the desk's polled state, marked ahead of
the guard. The refresh ends an idle family, clears the cookies and
answers SESSION_IDLE, so the web's refresh sees the code. Only this
device ends: the session version is not raised.
