feat(directory): show a space's announcements

A space's public page carries its running and upcoming announcements,
never an ended or a deleted one, by start then id, at most 20, each with
its start and end, so a visitor learns of a closure or an offer before
coming. They are read after the space's share lock: an announcement is no
fact group, and one posted between the two reads shows on the next.

PublicSpace gains a required field, so the web's one fixture typed by it
gains announcements: [] (worker B's licence for a shared type change).
