feat(subscriptions): list notices with extensions

The managed announcements list pages one state (running and upcoming
by start, ended by end, latest first) and shows each closure with the
extension of the subscriptions it covered: null until one is applied,
else its days, how many it moved and who applied it. Its summary counts
the three tabs and the closures started within 30 days with none.

The list is composed by subscriptions, which owns the extensions and
sits above announcements (the owner's Q2); the writes stay in
announcements, on the same path. The owner's overview will read the
same summary.
