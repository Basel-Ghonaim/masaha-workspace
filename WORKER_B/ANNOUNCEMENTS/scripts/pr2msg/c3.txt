feat(data-reports): let the owner decide reports

The owner lists the space's reports, by status and field, and never sees
who reported: the repository's select names no reporter, so no mapper can
show one. Reception is refused at the route before the query or the body
is read.

A decision resolves with a reply or none, or dismisses with one. It reads
the space's links under the space's lock, so an owner unlinked meanwhile
is refused, and stores who replied with it. A decided report is final:
another decision is REPORT_ALREADY_DECIDED, while the same decision
again answers as it is, so a retry after a lost answer is no refusal
(decisions 10, 11; S8). Each decision is audited.
