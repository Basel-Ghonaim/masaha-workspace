test(auth): prove a refresh renews no idle clock

A refresh copies the session's last activity into the new row, so a desk
that reloads or refreshes early still ends after 15 minutes idle. No test
refreshed a live reception session, so a rotation writing its own time
would have kept every desk alive (review of #118, point 1).

The test helper's sessions now carry a last activity far ahead, as their
expiry does, so a test clock ahead of real time never reads one as idle
(review of #118, point 4).
