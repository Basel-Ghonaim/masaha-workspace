feat(announcements): post and edit announcements

The staff (owner and reception) post an announcement and edit it.
A post carries an Idempotency-Key, unique within the space: reception
posts a closure on the spot, on a weak connection, and a retried post
must never make a second closure the owner could extend twice.

The migration announcement_key adds the key, backfilled for the rows
made before it; the tests and the demo seed that insert announcements
gain one. The period is held to its bounds (a start 30 days back to
365 ahead, an end after now and within 365 days of the start), and an
edit checks only what it changes, so an ended one stays correctable.
