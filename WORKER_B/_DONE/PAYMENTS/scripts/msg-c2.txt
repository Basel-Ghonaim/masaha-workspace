feat(payments): void a payment

The owner corrects a payment by voiding it with a reason (ADR 0010
rules 3-5): POST .../payments/:paymentId/void, refused to reception at
the route before the body is read. The desk takes the space's lock,
asks can() on the links under it, then locks the payment's row, so two
voids decide in turn; a second void answers the payment as it is, its
first reason kept. The void sets the row's void columns only, audited
payment.voided; every balance, debt, credit and price floor already
leaves voided payments out on read, so nothing else is rewritten.

The answer is the row the payments list will show: its item, the
item's customer, who recorded it and the void, named set-based
through visits, subscriptions (namesOf, new), customers and users.
