feat(payments): show a voided visit uncollected

A manual check-out with no customer is always paid in full, so the
uncollected visits were the auto-closed ones only. Once its payment is
voided such a visit owes again and showed in no list, debt or count:
the money vanished from every screen. The uncollected visits now also
hold a priced visit closed by hand with no customer that owes, where
the desk leaves it to a customer, takes its payment, or the owner
writes it off. One closed by hand unpriced stays the unpriced visits'
list (the owner's correction). Same query, same order and paging.
