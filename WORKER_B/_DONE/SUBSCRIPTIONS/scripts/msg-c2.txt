feat(desk): subscribe a customer

The desk makes a customer's subscription in one transaction under the
space's lock: from an offered package of the space, whose terms it
copies (ending validityDays after the start, both days included), or
from terms typed by whoever may type a price, with the reason recorded
beside who typed it. The start date is the one sent, else today.

A retry with the same key answers the first subscription as of now; an
archived customer is refused. The answer carries the subscription's
state and money and the customer's debt; payments come with renewals.

A subscription now shows the one it renews and its typed price, so the
customer file's exact answer gains both fields.
