feat(favorites): save and remove a favourite

A signed-in user keeps the listed spaces they want to find again (build
map step 10, decision 12): POST and DELETE /me/favorites/:spaceId, 204
either way, so a heart tapped twice or a retry is no error.

A save locks the user's row first (the session lock), so two saves at
199 cannot both pass the count of 200 (S9); a space already saved
answers before the listing and the ceiling are read. Whether a space is
listed is asked of the public listing, as the directory lists it (S12).
A removal deletes the caller's row only, listed or not.

FAVORITES_LIMIT_REACHED (409) is the new code, in both catalogues. The
service dates a favourite by the one clock, so its deps gain now.
