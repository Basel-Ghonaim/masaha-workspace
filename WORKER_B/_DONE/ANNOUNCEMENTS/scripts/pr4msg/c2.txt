feat(subscriptions): preview a closure's extension

Before extending, the owner sees the days the server proposes and how
many subscriptions the closure covered (decision 3 and 4): from the
Gaza day of its start to the day of its last instant, so an end at
midnight covers the day before (S4), to today while it runs; at most
365 days. The covered rule is one SQL fragment, so the extension will
move exactly what the preview counted. An applied extension is
answered instead, with how many it moved.

daysFrom moves from subscriptionStatus.ts to the shared Gaza time,
where closureSpan needs it too: one function, not a copy.
