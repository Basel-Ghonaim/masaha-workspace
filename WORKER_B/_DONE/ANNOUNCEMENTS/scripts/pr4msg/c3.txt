feat(subscriptions): extend after a closure

The owner moves the end of every subscription a closure covered by
the days typed (decisions 3-5, approved 2026-10-21): running on any
of its days, never one ended early or with no end, the preview's own
fragment. Once per closure (CLOSURE_ALREADY_EXTENDED) under one key,
unique on its own (S11); only once the closure has started; one
transaction locking the space, the closure, then the subscriptions by
id; audited closure.subscriptionsExtended. No undo: a wrong one is
corrected per subscription.

Reception is refused before the key or body is read, and can() is
asked again under the space's lock. Decision 4's rule replaces "all
active subscriptions" in the data model, the overview and ADR 0009.
