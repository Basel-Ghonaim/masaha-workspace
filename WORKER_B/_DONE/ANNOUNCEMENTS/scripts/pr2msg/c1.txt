feat(data-reports): report a space's wrong data

A signed-in user reports one field group of a listed space: 201 with the
open report, or 200 with the one already open for that field, so a double
tap files nothing twice. The space is found as its page finds it, the
user's row serialises their reports, and 10 reports an hour are counted
by the user before the body is read, repeats included (decision 9).

The migration data_report_rules adds who decided a report (resolved_as),
the partial unique index of one open report per user, space and field,
and CHECKs that keep a decision's fields together, a dismissal's reply and
an OTHER report's message, so the rules hold for any writer (S6, S7).

spaces gains namesWithDeleted, which the answer needs for the space's
names; its unit test's fake of the service gains the method.
