feat(data-reports): let the admin decide reports

The admin lists every report, by status, field and space, with who
reported and who decided by name and whether the space is verified now;
a soft-deleted space's reports are left out (ADR 0007).

The admin decides an unverified space's report only. The links are read
through space-links under the space's lock, so an owner linked while the
decision waits makes it 403, and an owner's decision committed first
makes the admin's REPORT_ALREADY_DECIDED. The reply is stored as
Masaha's.

The plan, the overview and the documentation map now list data reports
as built on the API (decision 10's wording in both).
