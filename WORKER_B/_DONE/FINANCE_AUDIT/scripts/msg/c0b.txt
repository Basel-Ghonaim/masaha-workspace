fix(subscriptions): refuse a key of another space

The extension's key is unique on its own, so findByKey finds an extension
of any space, and the retry compared only the closure. Another space's
owner, on their own URL with this closure's id and its key, was answered
201 with this space's extension: its days, its count and its applier
(#123's review, item 2).

findByKey now reads the closure's space, and a retry answers conflict
when the space or the closure differs, before anything is answered. A
conflict, not not_found: it is what a key reused on another closure of
the same space answers, so the key tells nothing of another space.
