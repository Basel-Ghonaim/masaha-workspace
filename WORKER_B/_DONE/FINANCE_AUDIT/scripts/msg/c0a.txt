fix(subscriptions): move unstarted renewals too

A closure's extension moved only the ends of the subscriptions it covered.
A renewal starts the day after the end it renews, so a renewal sold
before the extension was overrun by the moved end and the customer's
compensated days ran beside it (finding 151, #123's review).

The owner chose option A: the extension also moves each covered
subscription's renewal chain that has not started, start and end, by the
same days. The chain is read by one recursive query beside the covered
fragment and locked with the covered rows in one statement by id, under
the space's lock. Each renewal is linked to the extension, the preview
counts it, and the audit entry names the renewals apart (renewalIds).

Finding 151 is written and resolved here.
