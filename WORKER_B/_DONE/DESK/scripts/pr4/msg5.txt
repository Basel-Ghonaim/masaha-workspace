feat(desk): receive a payment for a visit

The desk can now take a payment for a closed visit after its check-out:
POST …/payments, its body naming the visit, with an Idempotency-Key.
The payment is inserted first, so the ledger's refusals answer their
codes before a phone is asked for; its trigger now also refuses a
written-off visit (VISIT_WRITTEN_OFF, through the translation table).
A retry answers the first payment with the sums as of now.

If part stays unpaid, one rule now serves the check-out and the
payment: a visit with a customer keeps it, and a phone that is not that
customer's is refused (VISIT_HAS_CUSTOMER); a visit with none needs a
phone. This resolves finding 100: an unpaid check-out no longer moves a
customer's debt to whoever the phone belongs to.
