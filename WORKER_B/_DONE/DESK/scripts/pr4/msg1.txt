fix(jobs): keep closing stays past a failing one

Carried from #97 (its review, findings 1, 4, 5, 6 and 3/7).

One stay that kept failing halted its job for every space, run after
run: the stays are read oldest first and nothing caught a stay's own
failure. Each stay is now closed in its own try: a failure is logged
with the stay's id and counted, the next stays are still closed, and
the job then fails so the cron service shows it. A space without its
settings row fails only its own stays (settingsFor leaves it out).

Documents: visits.md's boundary says which transactions are its own;
the scheduler's local timing has one home; .env.example no longer reads
as if the secret stopped the timer; the runbook names the header
exactly. Findings 111-113 record #95's nits 7 and 8 and #97's growing
uncollected read.
