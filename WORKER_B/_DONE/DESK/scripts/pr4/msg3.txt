feat(visits): store a visit's write-off

The owner may write off what a closed visit still owes (K6). The visit
keeps who, when and why in three columns, set together, never with a
blank reason, and only on a closed, priced visit: a CHECK, as the typed
charge's reason has. Undoing clears them; the audit keeps the history.

The payments trigger gains a fourth rule: a written-off visit takes no
payment (payments_visit_written_off). It lives in the ledger so every
insert path is covered, and the visit's row lock it already takes
decides a write-off racing a payment.

Every visit the desk is answered now carries writeOff, null unless
written off; the tests that compare a whole visit gain it.
