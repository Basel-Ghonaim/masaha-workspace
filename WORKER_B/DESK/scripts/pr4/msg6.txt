feat(desk): leave a visit's debt to a customer

«تسجيل دينًا» (DK-012): the desk can make an uncollected visit a
customer's debt by phone, with no payment, through PUT
…/visits/:visitId/customer. It is its own route rather than the payment
route with no amount, so nothing answers "created" for no row and no
idempotency key is spent: the same phone finds the same customer under
the space's lock, so a repeat changes nothing.

It uses the desk's one unpaid-part rule, so a visit that is already
another customer's is refused (VISIT_HAS_CUSTOMER); a written-off or
paid visit is refused too. The build map, the overview and the
documents now describe the step's whole debt flow.
