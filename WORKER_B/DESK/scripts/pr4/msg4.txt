feat(desk): write a visit off, and undo it

The owner may write off what a closed, priced visit still owes (K6):
POST …/visits/:visitId/write-off with a reason, DELETE to undo it. Only
the owner may (visits.writeOff in can(), at the route and again on the
links read under the space's lock). The balance is read under the
visit's lock, so a payment committed meanwhile is counted: only the rest
is written off, and nothing owed is refused (VISIT_ALREADY_PAID, new).
An unpriced visit is refused with VISIT_NOT_PRICED, whose lines become
general. A second write-off, or an undo of nothing, answers the visit as
it is, so neither needs an idempotency key.

A written-off visit leaves the uncollected visits. ADR 0009's table
gains the row; finding 110 records where the prototype disagrees.
