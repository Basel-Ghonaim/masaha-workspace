fix(visits): close every stay by its day's end

Carried from #97 (its review, finding 2; the owner chose (b), widened).

The data model calls a visit a same-day stay, but the stay limit could
carry a stay past midnight (23 h from a 20:00 check-in), and with the
closing check-out off and no limit a stay was never closed. A stay's
due time is now the earliest of the closing (when on), the stay limit
(when set) and 23:59 of its check-in's Gaza day, always.

This also bounds a charge to one day's stay, which resolves #95's
nit 9 (the charge overflowing its Int column).
