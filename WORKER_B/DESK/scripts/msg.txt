feat(payments): list uncollected visits

The visits the auto check-out closed and nobody paid in full are what
the owner's work queue will count. GET .../uncollected-visits answers
them, the unpriced ones included, with what was paid and what is still
owed, latest closed first, paged.

It is composed without repeating the balance (conventions §5, §9):
visits names the space's auto-closed visits with their charges, the
ledger sums their non-voided payments in one grouped query and decides
which are settled by its pure rule (balance.ts), and visits pages the
rest, so every page is full and the total right.
