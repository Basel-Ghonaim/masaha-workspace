feat(payments): list a space's payments

GET .../payments answers the payments of a range of Gaza days, the
latest received first, then by id, paged, filtered by who recorded
them, method, item kind and void (INCLUDE by default). Its meta
carries the totals of the whole filter but the void filter and the
page: received in all and by method, the count, and the voided ones
apart, so the ledger's footer and reception's cards come with the rows
in one request on the desk's weak connection.

The owner reads every payment; reception reads its own of today, the
service deciding with can() before any query, and a range or
recordedById from reception is refused rather than ignored. Neither
end means today; at most 92 days with both counted.
