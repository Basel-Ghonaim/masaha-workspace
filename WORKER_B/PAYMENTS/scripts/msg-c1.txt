feat(db): keep a written-off visit's payments

A write-off records what the visit still owed. Voiding one of its
payments afterwards would raise that debt while the visit stays
written off, so the owner would forgive money nobody decided to
forgive. The ledger's fourth rule now holds both ways, in the
database: payments_guard_change, copied from the payments migration,
refuses the void of a written-off visit's payment under the existing
constraint name payments_visit_written_off (VISIT_WRITTEN_OFF), until
the write-off is undone. No table changes and no new error code; the
code's two catalogue lines now cover a void too.
