# Oyoun Academy's logo — what it does (analysis for Masaha's wordmark, 2026-11-11)

Source: the Oyoun Academy LMS design system (claude.ai, the owner's company account) — `assets/Logos/OyounLogo.svg`
(the lockup) and `smallIcon.svg` (the monogram). **Studied for principles only; nothing is copied** (it is the
company's mark; Masaha is the owner's own project and must not read as an Oyoun product).

## What it is

- **A monogram "OA"**: a full circle (O) that runs into a tall triangle (A); the O's stroke becomes the A's left leg.
  The A has **no crossbar** — a short parallel stroke inside the right leg stands for it.
- **A two-line wordmark**: "OYOUN." in a light, geometric, wide-tracked capital; "ACADEMY" in a heavy, geometric,
  **oblique** capital beneath it.
- Colour: the monogram in the orange accent, the words in a muted blue (on navy); the monogram alone is the small icon.

## The principles behind it

1. **Geometric construction** — circle and triangle, a Futura-like skeleton: calm, rational, timeless.
2. **Two letters share a stroke** (a ligature): the O flows into the A — one shape, memorable, ownable.
3. **Negative space does the work** — the missing crossbar; the eye completes the A.
4. **Weight contrast inside the lockup** — light parent name, heavy product name: a clear hierarchy without colour.
5. **Capitals with generous tracking** — formal, institutional, readable at a distance.
6. **A full stop as a signature** — "OYOUN." reads as a confident statement.
7. **An oblique** for motion and energy ("ACADEMY").
8. **A monogram cut from the logo** for small sizes — the same system, not a second idea.

## What Masaha takes, and what it must not

- Takes: a geometric skeleton, one memorable letter-level detail (a shared stroke, a cut, or a mark of
  punctuation), restraint, a monogram derived from the word.
- Must not: an O-and-A circle-triangle monogram, the Futura-light + heavy-oblique pairing, the orange-on-navy, or
  "NAME." with a dot in the same way — anything that would read as a sibling of Oyoun's mark.
