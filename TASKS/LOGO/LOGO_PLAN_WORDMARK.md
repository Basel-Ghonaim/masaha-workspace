# Masaha logo — new direction: the wordmark "Masaha" (plan, 2026-11-11)

**The owner's decision (2026-11-11):** the symbol idea is dropped (rounds 1–2, the room-pin, are closed). **The logo
is the English word "Masaha"** — a wordmark, like Google, Stripe or Airbnb's word. The name «مساحة / Masaha» is
therefore the working brand name.

## The logic (why a wordmark works here)

- One element to learn instead of two: people remember the name, not a symbol.
- No pictogram to misread (a pin, a tile, a door) and nothing to clash with the map's status pins or the spaces'
  letter tiles.
- It carries the identity through **type, spacing and one spark accent** — the ink + spark system (IDENTITY I1–I3).

## Decisions to take (each with a recommendation)

- **W1 — one logo in both languages:** the Latin "Masaha" in the Arabic interface too (recommended: one mark,
  recognised everywhere; the Arabic name stays in titles, alt text and copy) · or an Arabic «مساحة» twin set in the
  same spirit.
- **W2 — case:** "masaha" lowercase (friendlier, rounder, a strong word shape: m-a-s-a-h-a has one ascender) ·
  "Masaha" (proper, formal). Recommended: decide by eye in round 1 — both are set.
- **W3 — type source:** a real, open-licensed (OFL) typeface, **adjusted** (spacing, one or two letters refined) —
  never hand-drawn letters from scratch. Candidates set in round 1: IBM Plex Sans (the product's own type), Inter
  Display, Manrope, Sora, Outfit, Plus Jakarta Sans, Lexend, DM Sans — at 600–800.
- **W4 — the spark accent (one, small, optional):** none (all ink) · a spark full stop "masaha." · the counter of
  the first "a" in spark · a spark bar under the "h". Recommended: decided in round 2, on the chosen typeface.
- **W5 — the small sizes:** a wordmark does not read at 16 px, so a **monogram** cut from the same letters ("m", or
  "M") is the favicon, the sidebar rail and the phone icon — drawn last, from the chosen wordmark.

## Rounds (each ends with one choice by the owner)

1. **Typefaces** — one sheet: the 8 candidates × lowercase / capitalised × weight, in ink, light and dark, at header
   size and large. The owner keeps 2.
2. **Detailing** — the 2 kept: letter spacing tuned by eye, the spark accent options of W4, in both identities
   (slate-coral, slate-berry), in place (site header, sidebar, Google sign-in, an email header). The owner keeps 1 +
   the identity.
3. **Monogram and files** — the monogram (16/32/48/180 px), the final SVGs (text converted to outlines, one fill,
   square viewBox for the monogram), the favicon set, `LOGO_DECISION.md` rewritten (rules: clear space = the
   x-height, minimum width, do and don't).
4. **Production** — a Work Item planned by the main supervisor with the identity's tokens (foundation + ADR, the
   header/sidebar, `apps/web/public/` favicons, Google Branding).

## Method and limits

- Drawn by the main supervisor in `TASKS\LOGO\wordmark\` as HTML + SVG previews (Google Fonts by link for
  rounds 1–2; for round 3 the chosen font file is downloaded, OFL, to convert the word to outlines — the download is
  asked first).
- Measured: ink on its grounds ≥ 4.5:1, the spark accent ≥ 3:1 on its ground; checked in protan/deutan.
- Nothing in the repository until round 4.
