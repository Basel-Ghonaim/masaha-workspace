# Masaha logo — the image-tool prompt

Assembled from the six settled axes in LOGO_NOTES.md. Use the **same prompt** in Recraft first, then in ChatGPT's
image tool. The tool makes **the mark only**; the words and lockups are set afterwards in Figma with the real font.

## Round 1 — explore

Run it several times, gather about ten variations, keep two or three. Shrink each kept one to 16 and 32 px before
going further.

```text
Design a logo mark (a symbol only, no text) for "Masaha", a calm, trustworthy,
practical web platform that lists coworking spaces in the Gaza Strip and shows
whether each space is available right now.

Concept: a single-line mark that fuses three meanings into ONE silhouette:
1) a location pin, 2) the Arabic letter Meem (م), 3) a work desk.
- The head of the pin is the head of the Meem: a soft rounded-square loop.
- The point of the pin is the Meem's descending tail.
- The tail lands on a short horizontal line that reads as a desk surface.
It must read as one connected shape, never as three icons placed together.

Style: geometric monoline with ONE thick stroke weight (about 1/8 of the mark's
width), rounded stroke ends, soft rounded corners, built on a square grid, the
whole mark fitting inside a square. Calm, practical, trustworthy. Not playful,
not decorative, not calligraphic. It must stay recognisable at 16 px.

Colour: solid black on a plain white background. Flat. No gradients, no shadows,
no texture, no 3D, no glow, no mock-up scene.

Avoid: a standard symmetric map marker, a house, a laptop or monitor, Wi-Fi or
signal arcs, a letter inside a filled square tile, dots, any text or other letters.

Also show a simplified version of each variation for 16–32 px: the pin and the
Meem only, with the desk line removed.

Show several distinctly different variations of this same concept.
```

## Round 2 — refine

Rerun with the round-1 prompt above it, and fill the brackets from what you kept.

```text
Keep the concept and every rule above.
Refine this variation: keep [what works, e.g. the loop's proportions];
change [what to fix, e.g. the tail is too thin / the desk line is too long].
Show four refinements of it, each with its simplified 16–32 px version.
```

## Before redrawing in Figma

- A native Arabic reader sees a «م» in it without being told.
- At 16 px the small version is still a pin and a «م», not a blot.
- It does not look like the directory map's pins, nor like a letter in a teal tile.
