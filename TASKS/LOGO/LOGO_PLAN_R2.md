# Masaha logo — plan, round 2 (2026-11-11): the mark in ink + spark

**Why now.** The colour identity moved from one teal to **ink + spark** (IDENTITY `0_OWNER_DECISIONS.md` I1–I3). The
owner likes both finalists equally — **`slate-coral`** and **`slate-berry`** — so **the logo decides**: the mark is
drawn in both and colour and logo are chosen together.

## What stands (LOGO_NOTES.md, not reopened)

- **Idea A, the room-pin** (2026-11-02): a rounded square (the workspace) with a pin's point (the location) and a
  door (open now). The «م» is not required. Round 1 drafts: `drafts/` b1 (outlined door), b2 (solid door), b3 (a
  diamond pin).
- **Form (axis 2):** the mark alone, a horizontal and a stacked lockup per language; a thick monoline (≈ 1/8 of the
  width), rounded ends, soft corners, a square grid; never mirrored; right of «مساحة», left of "Masaha".
- **Words (axis 4):** IBM Plex Sans Arabic / IBM Plex Sans, weight 600, every letter as the font draws it, no
  kashida — **set in the real font, never drawn by hand**.
- **Deliverables (axis 6):** eight black SVGs + the favicon set, in `final/`, names `masaha-…`.

## What changes (axis 3, colour — for the owner to settle)

The old rule "one teal for mark and word" no longer exists. The proposal: **the room in ink, the door in spark** —
the spark marks "what matters now", and the door means "open now": the same idea in the logo and in the interface.
The word is ink. One-colour versions stay (ink, white, black) for print and small sizes.

## Round 2 — what I draw (SVG, by hand, in `drafts/round2/`)

1. **Marks:** b1 and b2 refined on a 24-unit grid (stroke 3, corner radius from the stroke), plus **b4** — the door
   as a cut-out (negative space) instead of a filled shape, so the one-colour version keeps the door.
2. **Colours:** each mark in `slate-coral` and `slate-berry` (the trial's measured values: ink `#151F2C` / `#1A1F25`
   on light, `#F7FBFF` on dark; spark light `#D2573D` / `#B55BB3`, dark `#FEA793` / `#D27ED0`), light and dark, and
   one-colour ink.
3. **Sizes:** 16, 24, 32, 48, 96 px — the small mark checked at 16/32 (the door must still read; if not, the small
   mark is the room-pin without the door).
4. **In context:** the site header (horizontal lockup, Arabic and English), the dashboard sidebar's mark (expanded
   and on the rail), a browser tab (favicon), a Google sign-in card, a phone home-screen icon (ink mark on white),
   and **beside a space's letter tile and a map pin** — the logo must not look like either.
5. **Measured:** spark door on its ground ≥ 3:1 (graphic), ink ≥ 4.5:1; the door told apart from the room under
   protan/deutan simulation.
6. **Preview:** `drafts/round2/preview.html` (one page, light and dark, both identities side by side) and a PNG.

## Then

The owner picks a **mark + identity** together → round 3 refines only that one (spacing, clear space, minimum size)
→ `final/` files (lockups set from the real font, outlined; favicon set) → `LOGO_DECISION.md` rewritten → the
identity's foundation/ADR and the production Work Item (tokens + logo), planned by the main supervisor.
