docs(design): make berry the prototype's default

Production now draws ink + berry (ADR 0019), but the prototype still
drew Sea unless a page was opened with ?identity=slate-berry, so the
design source no longer showed what the site shows.

Without ?identity= (or with an unknown one) every app page is now
slate-berry, and a link carries the identity only when it is not the
default. Sea, the first identity, and slate-coral, the trial's other
candidate, stay behind ?identity=sea and ?identity=slate-coral to
compare; Sea is the bundle's own tokens, with no data-identity. The
tab's icon follows the identity as before. The reset email's design
takes the colours production sends, the stylesheet's header says what
is now the default, and the archive's documents follow: a CHANGES
section, INDEX's ?identity= row, and SCREENS' Wordmark line, whose
option A the logo superseded.
