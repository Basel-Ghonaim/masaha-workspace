feat(web): add the favicon and home-screen icon

The site had no icon of its own: a tab showed the browser's default, a
phone's home screen a picture of the page. It now shows the logo's small
mark.

favicon.svg is the berry mark of the design archive (its words path
defined once and drawn twice, with the mark file's clips and
transforms), ink and spark by class, switched to their dark values by
the browser's own scheme, with no background. favicon.ico holds 16, 32
and 48 px PNGs of the app icon (the mark on its white tile), for a
browser that takes no SVG and for a dark tab bar the ink would vanish
on. apple-touch-icon.png is the mark on white at 180 px, full bleed,
since iOS rounds the corners itself and turns transparent ones black.
The rasters were drawn from the owner's SVGs in headless Chrome at
device scale 1, the .ico packed by a small script; neither is kept.

The browser draws these icons and its bar's theme-color outside the
page, where no token reaches, so they carry the identity's hexes
themselves (ADR 0019). The new unit test holds the links, the files,
their sizes and those hexes, written by hand.
