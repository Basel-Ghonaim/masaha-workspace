feat(design-system): mark what matters in spark

The spark is the warm accent that marks what matters now, so it shows
only where something is singled out: the active line tab's indicator,
the map's selected mark (its ring, never a fill) and the item a user
lifts to reorder (the contacts' row and the showcase's items edged in
it, a photo outlined in it). It stays a fill, an edge or an outline,
never text or an action, so it keeps its weight where it appears.
