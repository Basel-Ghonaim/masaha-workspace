fix(desk): clear the search after a named visit

«تسجيل زيارة باسم «X»» from the desk's search opened the visit's
check-in with no word of what follows it, so after a successful
check-in the term and its groups stayed and the focus did not return
to the field, against S10. The search now hands its clear-and-focus
to the visit dialog, which runs it only once the check-in lands; a
failed one keeps the term.
