feat(desk): receive a payment on a customer's item

DK-012 at the desk: «استلام دفعة» records one payment on one item, since
the payment route pays one visit or one subscription (DI-22). Opened from
the page it lists the customers who owe, those present now first (DI-21);
from a customer's card («استلام {المبلغ}») or a subscriber's check-out
toast it opens on that customer, reads their file and offers its open
items: the unpaid visits, oldest first, then the subscriptions that owe,
the one the entry named chosen. The money block fills the amount with what
the item still owes and follows the item only while untouched (DI-35); a
usage-based subscription takes more, as credit. Each opening holds one
key (S4), and the toast names the item and what it still owes from the
answer (S8).
