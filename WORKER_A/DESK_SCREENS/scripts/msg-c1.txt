feat(occupancy): match the present to a search

K1's first group: the desk's one search lists the people present who
answer it before any customer. A person matches by a part of their name,
or, for a subscriber, by their phone's last two digits or more (the
phones of the open check-ins, passed in); a visit carries no phone, so
digits never find one. The group reads the same state now as the present
list, so it costs no request; the page sets each row's action, as it
does for the list. The present list's empty line now points to the
search too.
