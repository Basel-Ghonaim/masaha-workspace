docs(findings): record a paged list's silent wait

#101's review found that the next page of a paged list shows no sign of
loading: the shown rows stay, marked busy for assistive technology only,
and the pages still mark the old page as current. Every paged list on the
web behaves alike (the Directory and the admin's three lists), so it is
recorded as a design point for the owner rather than changed in one list.
