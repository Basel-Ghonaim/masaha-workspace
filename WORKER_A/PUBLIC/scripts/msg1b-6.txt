fix(directory): read a card's details

Each card is one link named by the space's name, and screen readers such
as VoiceOver on iOS and TalkBack read a labelled link by its name alone:
the live status, «موثّقة», the area and today's hours could be skipped,
so the status by word held for the eye only. The link keeps the name as
its name and is now described by each detail in turn (one reference
each, so two never run into one word), read after the name.
