feat(map): show many marks and pick a centre

The Directory's map needs many places on one map, a selected one, the
visitor's point and a point picked at a centre crosshair (U6, U7).
Leaflet stays in shared/map, held there by lint, so the map of many
marks is generic: its caller names each mark by shape and tone and its
meaning stays with the caller (S12); leaflet.css draws the marks, the
selected ring, the visitor's point and the crosshair from the tokens.
The tiles, controls and box of the point picker are shared with it, and
the line a map shows when it cannot load is its caller's.
