feat(directory): show the spaces on a map

Screen 2's map (U6): «قائمة | خريطة» switches the Directory to every
space that passes the filters as a pin, each live state a shape as well
as a colour, with its legend in the map. A chosen pin shows its card
from 768 px and, below it, a bottom sheet that is not modal, so the map
behind it still pans. Past 1,000 pins the map says so and asks again
within the box in view once it pauses. The view lives in the address
with the filters; clearing the filters keeps it. The list's failure
view is shared with the map's (useFailureView).
