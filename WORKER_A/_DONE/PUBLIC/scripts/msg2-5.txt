fix(directory): reset the price on clearing

Carried from #103 (item 6). The price filter keeps its typed texts and a
period chosen before any bound in its own state, reset only when the
address's bounds change: «abc» in «إلى», or «شهر» with no bound, stayed
after «مسح الفلاتر», and a bound typed within its pause came back after
the clear. Both clear actions now reset the fields and the period, which
also restarts the pause.
