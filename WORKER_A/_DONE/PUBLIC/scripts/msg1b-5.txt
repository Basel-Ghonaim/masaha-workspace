fix(site): focus the page after signing in

The site's shell and the focus shell are sibling layouts, so signing in
mounts a fresh site shell, whose focus rule took the page it opened on
for a first load and left the focus where it was: on the sign-in button,
which was gone, so on nothing. The shell now tells a load (a pop: the
first load, a reload) from an arrival by navigation, and on the latter
its first page takes the focus too.

Finding 130 gains the focus shell's own lack of the skip link and of the
focus move between its pages.
