feat(directory): list the spaces page by page

Screen 2's list: the site's «المساحات» was a placeholder over a built API.
A new web feature, directory, lists the publicly listed spaces 20 a page
from GET /spaces: each card one link with its cover or mark, its name,
its area (lookups' AreaName, set by the page), its live status by word,
«موثّقة» and today's hours on Gaza's calendar; no price. The search,
the order and the page live in the address (S5), a value the server
would refuse falling back to its default, and a search with no letter
to match kept off the request (S15). The cards stay while the next page
loads (S13); a new page takes the focus to the results. Foundation §5's
status roles follow DECISIONS_SHARED (S14).
