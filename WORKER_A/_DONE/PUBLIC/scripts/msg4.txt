feat(lookups): offer the public areas to the site

The site's Directory filters by place and names each card's area, and no
feature imports another, so lookups offers both and the page sets them
in the directory's slots (S7). AreaFilter chooses any number of areas
from the public catalogue, grouped under their governorates and found by
either name, with its summary, loading and failure. AreaName takes its
source: the public catalogue for the site, the admin's lists for the
space editor, which now says so.
