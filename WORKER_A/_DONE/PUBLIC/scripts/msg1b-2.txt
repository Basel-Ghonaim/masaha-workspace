fix(directory): keep the focus while searching

On page 2, a search typed after the pause replaced the address with page
1, and when the answer came the results' heading took the focus from the
field: the next keys were lost, and a phone's keyboard closed. The same
happened for an order chosen past page 1, and on a pasted page past the
last. The focus now moves only for a page the visitor chose: the pages
are links, pushed; a search, an order and a page past the last replace
the address.

The count's live region also announced the old total under the new
filters while the next answer loaded («21 مساحة مطابقة» before any
match). It is now worded from a real answer only, keeping its last line
meanwhile.
