feat(desk): void a payment

The owner voids a payment on the web (DK-016; ADR 0010 rules 3-5): a
payment's «⋯», named by its amount and who, opens a dialog that says the
void's true consequence and asks its reason. It is sent once with no
key, as the route answers a second void with the first; the written-off
visit's refusal, a payment gone and a 403 are each worded. The void is
the desk module's route, so it is desk's on the web, exported for the
payments page's rows (S1); a subscription's statement offers it to the
owner too (S10), so a payment on the wrong subscription is voided where
it is found.
