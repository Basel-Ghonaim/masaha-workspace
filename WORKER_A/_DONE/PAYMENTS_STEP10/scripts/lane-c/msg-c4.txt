feat(favorites): save a space from its card

PU-014 and U12: the heart on the Directory's and Home's cards and
beside a space's name. It flips at once and holds while its write is
pending (the one optimistic state, architecture §7); the save or the
removal is sent once with no key, said with «تراجع», or put back and
said with «إعادة المحاولة» (DI-49); a full list offers «إدارة المفضّلة».

The saved state is the directory's: the user's favourites, read once
per signed-in visit, tell each card and the page whether the space is
saved; a write reads the whole ['me'] scope again (S13). The card's
action stands beside its link, never inside it. A guest's press holds
the favourite in memory (S14) and the page asks them to sign in; the
site's shell saves it once they are back (C4).
