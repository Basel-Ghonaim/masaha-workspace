feat(announcements): extend after a closure

O11 on #123's API (S11, DI-19, DI-20): on a closure that has started
and is not extended, the owner's «تمديد الاشتراكات» reads the preview
at each opening - how many subscriptions the extension moves now (those
the closure covered and, since #128, their renewals not started) and
the closure's days - then sends the days typed, with one key per
opening. A failure stays in the dialog with the days kept; a closure
that covered none offers nothing (S11). Reception reads that the owner
extends; an extended closure says how many, how many days, by whom,
when.
