feat(announcements): end, start or delete one

O10 (DI-16 to DI-18): each card's actions by its state - «إنهاء الآن»
on a running one, acting at once with «تراجع» in its toast (one PATCH
of the old end, not offered on an extended closure, whose end may only
come earlier), «ابدأ الآن» on an upcoming one, and «حذف…» in the «⋯»,
which asks first, naming the announcement and the true consequence of
its state. An extended closure says why it has no delete.

Each write is idempotent by design: sent once with no key, never again
by itself (S4). When the card holding the focus leaves its tab, the
next card's first action takes it, else the tab shown (DI-24).
