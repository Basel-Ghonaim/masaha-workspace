feat(data-reports): decide a space's reports

Screen 23 (O12): the owner's «بلاغات البيانات» lists the space's reports
by «الكل · جديدة · تمت المراجعة» (the chip and the page in the address),
the reporter a visitor and the day only, never the time (finding 150).
One decision sheet decides each: «تم التصحيح» with an optional reply,
«لم يُعتمد» with a required one, focused when missing; sent once with
no key, REPORT_ALREADY_DECIDED, a 404 and a 403 each worded; a decided
report read-only. The toast says the report went to «تمت المراجعة».

The feature is any page group, its repository, keys, index and copy
section shaped so the admin's and the user's parts are added beside
the owner's.
