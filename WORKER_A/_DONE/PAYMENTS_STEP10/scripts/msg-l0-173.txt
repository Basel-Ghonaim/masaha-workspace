fix(design-system): start a range on each click

react-day-picker 10 ends a range on its first click: with no
resetOnSelect, a click on no range gives from and to the same day, and
a click on a whole one extends it. The layer's comment said otherwise,
and the ledger's period met it in #127's review (finding 173).

The range Calendar now sets resetOnSelect, which a caller may still set:
the first click leaves to empty, the second ends the range, a third
starts afresh.
