### F1 — should-fix · The period's calendar applies a range on its first click
- Where: `apps/web/src/features/payments/components/ledger/LedgerFilters.tsx:89-101` at 27bcd1a0 (`<Calendar mode="range" … onSelect>`). The comment at `:62-66` says "a range is applied once both its ends are chosen".

With no `min` (it defaults to 0) and no `resetOnSelect`, a click on an empty selection returns `{ from: day, to: day }`. A click on a full selection extends it from its `from` (`{ from, to: day }`) instead of starting a new range. The layer's `Calendar` sets neither prop, and its `DateRange` comment ("`to` is missing until the second click", `Calendar.tsx:10`) is wrong for this version. No test clicks the ledger's calendar: test 17 only checks that the trigger is there. - Scenario: the owner, on today's ledger, opens «الفترة» and clicks 1 October to start 1–21 October. The popover closes at once and the ledger and its totals show 1 October alone. The owner has to reopen it and click 21 October. Starting from 1–21 October, a click on 5 October (meaning to start 5–10) applies 1–5 October and closes. There is no way to choose a new range in one opening. The money stays true because the totals line names the period it shows, but the main control of screen 17 does not do what DI-2 and §9 describe.
- Fix: pass `resetOnSelect` to the range `Calendar` in `RangeField`. A click on a full or empty selection then starts `{ from, to: undefined }`, the second click completes it, and two clicks on the same day still give one day. Correct the comment at `:62-66`. Add a component test in `PaymentsLedger.component.test.tsx` that opens «Period», clicks two days, expects the address `?from=…&to=…`, and expects the picker to stay open after the first click. Its break: `resetOnSelect` dropped. · Belongs: a fix round on this PR (the layer's false `DateRange` comment as finding 173, F5).
