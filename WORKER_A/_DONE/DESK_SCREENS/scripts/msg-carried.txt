fix(desk): show the last uncollected page in range

A page of the uncollected visits past the last (its last visits collected
or written off, or a stale address) showed an empty card that read
«لا شيء لتحصيله» and hid the rows of the pages before it and their
pagination. The card now keeps its page in range with usePageInRange, as
the other paged lists do: the last page is shown in its place and the
address replaced, not pushed; the card reads empty only when the list's
total is 0.

Carried from #117's review (F1).
