feat(desk): edit, archive and restore a customer

Each customer listed — a row of «الزبائن», a card of the desk's search,
the file's header — gains their «⋯», named by them (DI-39): «ملف
الزبون», «تعديل البيانات» and the owner's «أرشفة…», destructive (DI-46).
The edit sends only the fields that differ from the customer as loaded,
a cleared phone as null, nothing when none does, and carries no key, so
it is never re-sent by itself: a late retry cannot put back what another
desk changed (finding 123). The owner archives a settled customer at
once with «تراجع», asks first for one who owes, naming the debt, and is
told a present customer must check out first (DK-014, P9, DI-47); an
archived one shows «استرجاع». Reception is offered neither.
