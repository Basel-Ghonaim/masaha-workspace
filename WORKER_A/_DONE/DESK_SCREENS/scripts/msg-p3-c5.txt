feat(session): say a session ended for idleness

The server now ends a reception session left idle for 15 minutes and
answers 401 SESSION_IDLE, the refresh too (#118, the owner's revision
of open decision 4). The session store keeps why the last session ended
beside its status: endedBy is idle when a refresh, the restore's or the
transport's, is refused with that code, and is cleared once a session
is established. The sign-in page says the error's catalogue line above
its form while it holds, so a receptionist back at the shared desk
knows why they must sign in again.
