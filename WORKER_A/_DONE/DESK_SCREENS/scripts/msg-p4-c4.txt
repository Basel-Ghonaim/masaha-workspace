feat(desk): renew a subscription with its debts

K3 and DK-007: «تجديد» on a customer's latest subscription opens the sheet
for its renewal, which starts where the statement says, offers the
renewed package as one line with «تغيير الباقة» or repeats its custom
terms, and sends renewalOfId over the customer's earlier debts. A
subscription renewed meanwhile is said and the sheet makes a new one.

Spec D (#118) on the web: the space's published week and month prices
are offered first under «منشورة للزوار», each sent as published { period,
audience, shiftId?, name }, never a price id. The prices are read again
each time the sheet opens, the toast states the price the server
charged, and a refusal for the price reads them again (finding 171, the
supervisor's decision for the owner to confirm; the finding stays open).
