feat(desk): check subscribers in and out

Step 8's check-ins at the desk. A customer's «حضور» checks them in on a
current subscription that runs in one tap, with one key until it lands;
on none, an expired one or one not started - what the row already says -
a choice comes first: a visit by their name, which is not added to their
file (finding 160), or the check-in anyway (DI-14). Every other warning is
the server's, said after in the toast (S6); MEMBER_ALREADY_CHECKED_IN says
they are here. A coming subscription's start is read from the customer's
file, since the search's row carries its end only.

A subscriber's «خروج» is one tap with no key (S3): the toast says the stay
and what the subscription still owes, from the answer, and the button keeps
the focus so the list hands it on once the row leaves (DI-31). The open
check-ins give the search each subscriber's phone and the present list
their subscription's first progress line. After a check-in or a check-out
started from the search, it is cleared and takes the focus (S10).
