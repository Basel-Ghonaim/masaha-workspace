feat(jobs): sweep expired counters and tokens

A session nobody refreshes again, a reset link never used, a recovery
whose browser never came back and a rate-limit key never hit again kept
their rows for good (finding 17). Two timed jobs join the auto
check-outs: sessions.sweepExpired deletes the refresh tokens, reset
tokens and recoveries that ended, and rateLimits.sweepExpired the
counters whose window ended, at most 5,000 rows a table a run, so a run
stays within the function's time. Each condition is checked again on
the row deleted, so a key hit at that moment keeps its new window.
