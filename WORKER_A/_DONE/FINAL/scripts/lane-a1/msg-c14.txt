feat(auth): free an expired account's address

An address held by an account whose temporary password expired unused
stayed locked out until the admin acted (finding 91). Registering it,
or a first Google sign-in at it, now replaces that account, in the new
account's transaction, only when it never acted (no audit entry as its
actor, no favourite, no data report) and holds no active owner link
(S1): its reception link ends by the system, its sessions end, it is
suspended, its address becomes replaced-{id}@replaced.invalid, and
user.replaced is audited. auth orchestrates; whether a person acted is
the audit reader's, a level above, passed in as a port.
