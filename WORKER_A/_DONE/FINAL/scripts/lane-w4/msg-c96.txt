feat(auth): keep a guest's action through a reset

A guest's held favourite or report lived in memory only, so a password
recovery, whose reset link opens a new tab, lost it (finding 168). It
is now also kept in the browser's storage for its 15 minutes, never in
the address. Only a sign-in in this browser confirms it, never a
restore: the app goes back to the page it was pressed on, where the
favourite is saved or the report's dialog opens. It is let go when it
runs, when the guest lands on another site page, and on a sign-out.
