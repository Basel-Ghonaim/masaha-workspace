feat(users): edit one's own account

Screen 9, the account's settings at /me, was a stub. It reads GET /me
and draws three cards: the profile (the name saved with PATCH /me, the
held session taking it so the account menu shows it at once), the
preferences (the language applied at once and saved to the account,
put back with a retry when the save fails; the theme on this device
only, DI20), and the ways of signing in (Google linked, the password
change under Change, or a Google-only account's link to set one; C1,
C2, C6). The session gains accountEdited, as passwordChanged.
