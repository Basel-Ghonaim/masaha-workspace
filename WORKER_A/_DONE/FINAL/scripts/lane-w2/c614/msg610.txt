feat(data-reports): open a report beside its field

An owner or the admin reached a report's field from its sheet with no
trace of the report there, and the list could not be opened on one
report (G20.1, G21.3, G23.1, G26.7, G28.2). The editor also opened at
its top: its cards draw only after the space's read, so the address's
anchor found nothing (finding 184).

- `data-reports` reads one report by its id (C2.14's routes, S5) and
  exports `ReportNotice` and `useReportNotice`: the report `?report=`
  names draws above its field's section, «بلاغ مفتوح على {field}»,
  its words with «زائر» to the owner and the reporter's name to the
  admin, «تم التصحيح» resolving it in place, and «فتح البلاغ للرد»;
- `SpaceEditor` takes a page's notice above a section (`noticeFor`);
  the owner's profile, Packages & prices and the admin's editor set it;
- each sheet's «تعديل الحقل» carries the report
  (`?report={id}#{section}`), and both lists open `?open={id}`, read by
  its id when the page does not show it;
- the editor lands on the section its hash names once drawn
  (`useHashLanding`), the cards' headings focusable.
