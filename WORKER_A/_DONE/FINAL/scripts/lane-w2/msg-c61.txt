feat(audit): show a space's audit log

The owner's audit log had a route and a title but no screen (finding
14). O13 defines it as the admin's audit page scoped to the space, so
one feature, `audit`, draws both logs (D6.1, DI1); this commit builds
it with the owner's page.

- The log's address is its state: the target's search, the period
  (the last 30 days or all time, DI17), the actor and one group of
  actions, sent as their list (at most 30), and the page.
- Each entry: the Gaza date with its year and the time, the actor (a
  person by name, «فريق مساحة» for the admin, «النظام» for a job), the
  action's words, red for a destructive act, the target with a link
  where the page gives one, and the details only where the stored
  values say something (DI18), each field read as its writer stores
  it (EC12).
- The actor filter shows only while the period has more than one
  actor; the filters sit in a sheet below 768 px (P5).
- The owner's page names the space from the switcher's read and links
  a customer, the space, a package, an announcement, a payment and a
  report to their pages.
