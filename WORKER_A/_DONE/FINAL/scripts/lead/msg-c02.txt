feat(shared): refuse a published price changed

The desk will send the published price it showed when it subscribes a
customer from it, and the server will refuse when the price changed
since (the owner's finding 171 (a)). This commit adds the domain code,
PUBLISHED_PRICE_CHANGED (409), its words in both catalogues, and its
line in the contract's §6; the subscribe's comparison and the sheet's
handling come in their own commits.
