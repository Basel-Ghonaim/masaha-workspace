feat(desk): say a usage subscriber's due at out

A usage-based subscriber's check-out was one tap whose toast said what
the subscription owed, with no way to take it there and then (G13.4).
No quote exists for an open check-in and the web never prices, so the
due is said after the close, from the server's answer (DI10): a dialog
with today's stay and what was paid, what the subscription still owes,
and the money block over it, «استلام {amount}» recording it on the
subscription with its own key, «لاحقًا» leaving it owed. A fixed-price
subscriber stays one tap with its toast.
