feat(desk): correct a subscription from its card

A subscription's corrections had routes and no screen (finding 163).
Its card's «⋯» in the file now offers «تصحيح الاشتراك» (DI11), held
with «انتهى الاشتراك مبكرًا» on one ended early (DI13). One end sheet
(DI12): the name, dates and limits for the staff, the owner's price
with why. «حفظ التصحيح» sends only the changed fields, the terms
first, then the price, each its own outcome, so terms saved before a
refused price are not sent again. Its refusals stay in the sheet.
