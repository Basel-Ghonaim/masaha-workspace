fix(desk): focus the write-off's reason first

An alert dialog gives its first focus to «رجوع», and in a browser a
field's autoFocus loses to it, so the write-off's reason was not where
the keyboard was (finding 172). The dialog now places the focus on the
reason itself, as the void's does, and the field's autoFocus is gone.

Its test drops a field's autoFocus, as a browser does, so only the
dialog's own placing of the focus can pass.
