feat(desk): renew or subscribe from the desk

A search card said nothing of how far its subscription had gone, nor
whether its customer was here, and the desk could not renew from it.
A card now lists each limit with what was used of it, says «غير
حاضر», and offers «تجديد» while its subscription ends soon or has
expired, reading its statement for the state the sheet renews. The
pre-check-in choice adds «تجديد» for an expired subscription and
«اشتراك جديد» for none.
