feat(design-system): reach a held menu item

Radix's menu leaves a disabled item out of its roving focus and its
typeahead (focusable: !disabled in @radix-ui/react-menu 2.1.24), so a
row menu's held action, drawn disabled with its reason inside, is
never reached by the keyboard and its reason never read (finding 105).

DropdownMenuItem gains held: the item is aria-disabled, drawn at 50%
like a disabled one, and its selection is prevented, but it is not
disabled, so the arrow keys and typeahead reach it. The staff and
users menus switch to it in their own commits, which close the
finding. The foundation's DropdownMenu row says it.
