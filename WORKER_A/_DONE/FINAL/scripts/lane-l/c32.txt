feat(design-system): draw bar charts and a heatmap

Finance (screen 19) draws income by month and its busy hours on the
chart-1…5 roles. No chart library is worth its weight for four charts,
so the layer gains two hand-built ones in its main entry:

- BarChart: stacked bars at 200px or 120px, a tick under each bar and
  a legend when there are two series or more.
- Heatmap: days by hours, each cell primary at one of five steps over
  muted, with a legend from fewer to more.

Both draw for the eye only; a visually hidden table holds every value,
so assistive technology reads numbers rather than shapes. The sizes
and the steps are component tokens, named in the foundation with the
two inventory rows; the showcase shows both.
