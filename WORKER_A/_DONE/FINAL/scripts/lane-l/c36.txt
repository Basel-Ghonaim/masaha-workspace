feat(lists): mark the page asked while it loads

A tap on a page of a paged list changed nothing on screen until the
answer came: ListPagination read the shown answer's page, and the
rows stayed as they were, marked only aria-busy (finding 132). The
design's quiet sign (DI4) is two parts:

- ListPagination marks the page the address asks for current at once,
  falling back to the answer's page when the address names none.
- DataTable takes refreshing, and the layer gains Refreshing for a
  list that is not a table: the rows or cards shown stay, dimmed by
  the refreshing token (60%), and the list is marked busy.

Each paged list passes its placeholder state in its own commit. The
foundation names the wrapper, the prop and the token; the showcase
shows the wrapper.
