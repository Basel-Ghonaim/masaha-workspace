feat(spaces): name the retired amenities held

A space keeps a retired amenity's link until a save leaves it out, but
its reads gave only the ids, so the owner's checklist could not show
the retired ones it holds (finding 99).

AdminSpace (the admin's and the managed reads, and every fact save's
answer) carries retiredAmenities: those of amenityIds the platform
retired, with their names and icon, in the platform's order, named by
lookups in the read's own transaction.
