test(db): pin Gaza's transitions in PostgreSQL

Gaza's dates are read by two zone databases: Node's, through the shared
calendar, and PostgreSQL's, in every AT TIME ZONE of the money and the
attendance (finding 120). A test finds each 2026 change of Gaza's
offset with Node's zone data and asserts that PostgreSQL reads the same
Gaza date and minute an hour before, at and an hour after it.
