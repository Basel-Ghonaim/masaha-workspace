feat(db): compute a subscription's due in bigint

subscription_usage computed a usage-based subscription's due in
integer: a per-day subscription at the price's bound overflowed after
about 215 days, and one such row answered 500 to the space's whole
customers screen (finding 122).

The migration drops and recreates the function with the due in bigint.
The sums of debt and credit built on it are bigint too; every reader
maps them through Number(), exact within the price's bound.
