feat(visits): check a visit in for its customer

The desk's «تسجيل كزيارة» knows the customer it checks in, but a
visit's check-in took a name only, so the visit belonged to no one
until a phone was given at its check-out (finding 160).

The check-in takes customerId. Under the space's lock the desk checks
the customer is the space's (invalid_choice), not archived
(CUSTOMER_ARCHIVED) and not present (MEMBER_ALREADY_CHECKED_IN), as a
subscriber's check-in does; the visit is created with its customer,
and its audit entry names it.
