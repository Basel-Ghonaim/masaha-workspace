feat(db): keep a subscription's published period

The customers list's filter names «أسبوعي» and «شهري» (foundation §13
#14), but a subscription sold from a published price kept no period:
a custom seven-day one looks the same.

Migration subscription_published_period adds published_period, set to
WEEK or MONTH by a subscribe from a published price, with a CHECK that
allows only those two and never with a package. SubscriptionState
answers publishedPeriod; the customers query's kind takes WEEK and
MONTH, and CUSTOM narrows to neither a package nor a published price.
The web's kind filter gets the two choices' words, so it stays typed.
