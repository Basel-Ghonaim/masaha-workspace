fix(customers): match a phone under either code

A whole phone typed at the desk matched a phone stored under the other
country code by its national digits with the trunk 0 at its end. That
worked only because 970 ends in 0: 0591234567 never found a stored
+972591234567 (finding 126).

The search now matches the national digits without the trunk 0, nine
at least, at the end of the stored digits, so +970 and +972 find each
other both ways.
