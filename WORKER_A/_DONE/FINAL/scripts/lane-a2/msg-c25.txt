feat(jobs): reclaim the files no photo holds

A photo's files are stored before its row and removed after its row
is deleted, so a crash, a timed-out upload or a failed removal leaves
files no row names, and nothing reclaimed them (finding 63).

The storage port lists the files under a prefix with their dates: the
local disk by walking its folder, Cloudinary by its Admin API, page by
page. A timed job, photos.reclaimOrphans, acts in Gaza's 03:00 hour
only: it removes each photo file no row holds and older than an hour,
at most 200 a run, each logged [storage:reclaimed].
