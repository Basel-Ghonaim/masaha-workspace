feat(data-reports): read one report by its id

The report notice opens one report from the address; searching for it
in the list's first page could miss it (S5).

GET /manage/spaces/:spaceId/data-reports/:reportId answers the owner
one of the space's reports, as the list shows it and never with its
reporter (reception refused before the read); GET
/admin/data-reports/:reportId answers the admin one report. Another
space's, an unknown one or a deleted space's is not_found.
