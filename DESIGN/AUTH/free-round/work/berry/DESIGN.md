# Berry («توت»)

**The colour owns the page, and the people sit at one long table.** There is no white page: the whole ground is the
logo's berry. The title «تسجيل الدخول» is set in Kufam Black at monumental size, a heavy geometric Kufi that is a close
cousin of the logo's letters. The form is the only paper, one sheet laid on the colour. Along the bottom runs a long
drawing of one shared work table: a man pouring tea for everyone, a woman in a scarf at her laptop, a student in
headphones, a man drinking his tea, the router, a woman with glasses and a bun, a guy on the phone, a woman in a light
scarf waving hello, a man thinking with his chin in his hand. Every device is plugged into one of three power strips on
the table's front, and every socket is taken.

Open `index.html`. The page reads its state from the address: `?lang=ar|en`, `?theme=light|dark`,
`?state=wrong|toomany|offline|submitting|google|session|fields`, `?from=<space>` (for example `?from=elevate`).

## The berry's role

**The berry is the ground.** It is not an accent anywhere because it is everything. That forces the honest question
the brand's colour raises: the berry `#B55BB3` is a mid-tone, so the brand's ink `#1A1F25` reaches only 4.04 : 1 on
it, and white only 4.11 : 1. This direction answers by deepening the ink one step to the system's own n-950,
`#0D1217`, which holds 4.6 : 1 on the berry. So every word on the colour, even the 16 px owners' note, passes AA, and the
page is drawn in exactly the logo's two colours: ink on berry. At night the room goes dark plum (s-950) and the laptop
screens light the faces.

Three reading sizes (16, 18, 20) and one title. Kufam carries the voice; Plex, the product's own text face, carries
everything you read, so only the voice changes.

## Spacing and layout

8 px grid; gaps of 8, 16, 24 and 32. Desktop: a bar (logo on its tab at the start, switches at the end), then a grid with
the title and the way back in the start column, the way in for new people under them, and the 464 px sheet in the end
column, its top level with the title's. In the DOM the sheet comes before the new-people block, so the keyboard reaches
the form first; the grid puts the block back on the start side. The table runs the full width at the bottom
(`clamp(160px, 16.4vw, 238px)` tall), and the privacy link sits on the table's front between two power strips. Phone
(≤ 960 px): one column in this order: way back, title, lede, sheet, new people, privacy, then the table, cropped to its
middle (the student in headphones, the man with his tea, the router, the woman with the bun, and one whole power strip).

## Surfaces and shape

One radius family: 16 px for fields, notes and the logo's tab, 32 px for the sheet, full pills for every button and
switch. One surface, the sheet, with no shadow (paper on colour needs none). Buttons are 56 px; switches 44 px pills
outlined in the ink.

## The drawing

Generated by `work/tools/frieze.py` into inline SVG (13 KB), so it costs no image request. Thick round line (3 px), a
slight hand wobble from one SVG turbulence filter on the people only (the table and cables stay crisp). The people sit
at different heights and widths, with noses and brows where they help, so they read as people rather than one figure
repeated. Everyone is drawn in the same two inks, which keeps the drawing about the scene (work going on, tea going
round, every socket taken) rather than about anyone's skin. Three of the laptops carry the ة as a sticker: the logo,
in the product's world. The scarves are drawn as cloth (one dark, one light with its folds), and every face is calm or
smiling.

## Components

- **Switches:** 44 px pills outlined in the ink; hover fills them.
- **Google:** Google's own button, pill shape (Google offers it), outline in light, filled black in dark; 40 px.
- **Fields:** 56 px, 16 px radius, label above, error below with an icon, the email typed left to right; validation on
  blur; the error clears when fixed; a failed submit moves focus to the first wrong field.
- **Sign in:** a 56 px ink pill. Busy: spinner, «جارٍ تسجيل الدخول…», `aria-busy`, same size.
- **«إنشاء حساب»:** a paper pill with an ink ring on the colour, so the second way in is as visible as the first; at
  night it is outlined, so it never outshines Sign in.
- **Alerts:** the error tint, 16 px radius, `role="alert"`, focus moves to it.
- **Small links** have a 44 px hit area drawn outside the text, so nothing moves.

## Motion

None on load. At night only, the router's second light blinks slowly (2.4 s), and not at all under reduced motion.

## Accessibility

Every word on the berry is n-950 at 4.6 : 1 (AA for body text, with little margin: this is the colour's limit, see the
weakest point in the report). On the sheet, text is 15.9 : 1 and secondary text and placeholders 5.8 : 1. The focus
ring is the ink on the berry (4.6 : 1) and on paper (18 : 1); paper at night. Targets ≥ 44 px. Keyboard order,
checked by tabbing in Chrome: logo, language, theme, way back, Google, email, forgotten password, password, show,
Sign in, Create account, Contact us, privacy. The drawing is `aria-hidden`.

## What it keeps from the product's design system, and what it changes

**Keeps:** every colour is from the system's ramps (s-500, s-950, s-400, s-100, s-900, n-50…n-950, r-50 / r-600,
r-400 / r-950); IBM Plex Sans Arabic for all reading text; the logo uncoloured at 40 px, never mirrored (on the berry it
sits on a paper tab rather than being recoloured); Western digits; logical properties; 16 px+ typed text; submit stays
enabled; links in sentences underlined; focus to the first invalid field.

**Changes, and why:**
- *The spark as the ground.* The system uses the spark "sparingly, never as text, never on an action". This direction
  turns that upside down on purpose, the way the owner's Tabby reference does with its green: the brand's colour is
  the page. It stays a sign-in page; the system's rules still hold on the sheet.
- *Text on the spark.* Words sit directly on the berry, in n-950, because n-950 is the one ink in the system that reaches
  4.5 : 1 there.
- *Tone.* The system asks for "calm, no playful or decorative styling" for the product's daily tools. A sign-in page is
  a threshold, not a tool, and this direction spends its warmth there; the dashboard behind it would stay calm.
- *Display type.* Kufam 900 at up to 148 px; the system's largest style is 44 px. Only for this title.
- *Shape and size.* Pills, 16 px fields and a 32 px sheet instead of 6–8 px radii; 56 px controls instead of 40–44:
  rounder and bigger, like the logo's letters.

## Weight

About 64 KB of HTML including the inline logo and the 13 KB drawing; no images. Fonts are the heaviest of the three
directions (Plex 400 and 600 Arabic, about 87 KB, plus Kufam's Arabic, 34 KB). In production, Kufam should be requested
with Google's `&text=` for the title's letters only (a few KB), which brings this direction close to the others.
