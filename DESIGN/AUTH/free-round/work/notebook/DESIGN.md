# Notebook («كرّاسة»)

**A student's squared notebook, written by hand.** The page is a sheet of squared paper: the 8 px grid made visible
as a 16 px ruling, with a double berry margin rule on the side where the writing starts, the right in Arabic, the way
Arabic exercise books are ruled. On a wide screen it is an open spread, and the two pages are the two ways in: the
first page is for people coming back and holds the form; the facing page is for people new to Masaha, with the
owners' note and a pen sketch of a real work desk. The title is written, not typeset: «تسجيل الدخول» in Ruqaa, the
everyday hand taught in schools across the region, underlined twice in berry the way a lesson's title is. Every line
of print stands on a rule.

Open `index.html`. The page reads its state from the address: `?lang=ar|en`, `?theme=light|dark`,
`?state=wrong|toomany|offline|submitting|google|session|fields`, `?from=<space>` (for example `?from=elevate`).

## The berry's role

**The berry is the pen.** It writes: the margin rules, the double underline under the title, the margin note
«لأصحاب المساحات» that labels the owners' paragraph, the line beside a note, the one primary button (like a stamp),
the focus ring, the logo's dots, and in the sketch the lantern's light. Everything printed is ink. Errors are the other
pen, red, the way a teacher marks a page. So colour always means "a hand did this here".

## Type

| Role | Face | Size / line height |
|---|---|---|
| title (h1) | **Aref Ruqaa 700** (Arabic) · **Kalam 700** (English) | 88 / 112 (English 80) · phone 64 / 96 |
| facing page's title (h2) | the same hand | 56 / 96 · phone 44 / 64 |
| margin note, error title | the same hand | 22 / 32 (note, berry, turned −4°) · 28 / 32 (error, red) |
| print: lede, labels, fields, buttons, the owners' note | **Markazi Text** 400 / 600 | 21 / 32 |
| small print: the way back, links, errors, «أو», privacy | Markazi Text | 18 / 32 |

Two voices: the hand (Ruqaa, the hand people actually write in; in English, Kalam, an adult hand with weight) and the
print (Markazi, a naskh made for reading, with a Latin cut drawn to match). Two print sizes and one leading. Markazi's
letters are small for their size; 21 px Markazi reads like about 17 px of most Arabic sans faces.

## The ruling, and text that stands on it

The squares are 16 px (two steps of the 8 px grid) and every line of print is 32 px (two squares). Every block's top
and height is a multiple of 16. The ruling is shifted 5 px down, because Markazi at 21 / 32 sets its baseline 21 px into
the line, so every line of print stands on a rule. The two titles are nudged a few pixels (`--h1-nudge`,
`--h2-nudge`, measured per language and per width in Chrome) so they stand on a rule too, and both pages' titles share
the same rule on a wide screen. Google's 40 px button keeps 4 px above and below so it fills three squares.

## Spacing and layout

Gaps of 16 and 32 (and 48 once, before the owners' note). Desktop: two equal pages and a fold between them. Each page
has a 96 px margin strip on its start side, then 48 px, then a 400 px column, the width of Google's button. The
switches sit at the end of the first page's header, where they are in the DOM. Phone (≤ 960 px): one page after the
other, the margin narrowed to 32 px; the facing page begins under a soft page edge.

## Surfaces and shape

Paper only. No cards, no shadows except the fold. Controls are drawn: 1.5 px ink borders and 3 px corners, as if ruled
with a pen. Google's button keeps Google's own shape.

## The sketch

Generated by `work/tools/sketch.py` into inline SVG (6 KB): a lantern with its ring handle and a bulb in its glass,
light drawn around it in the berry pen; a laptop with Masaha open; a glass of tea with a sprig of sage; a phone charging;
and under the desk a power strip with every socket taken, two of them feeding the neighbours. Ink line with a slight pen
wobble (one turbulence filter), berry only on the logo's dots, the tea, the lantern's light and switch and the strip's
light.

## Components

- **Switches:** 48 px, drawn boxes, ink on paper; hover inverts.
- **Google:** Google's own button, rectangular, outline in light, filled black in dark; 40 px.
- **Fields:** 48 px (three squares), label above, error below in red print with an icon, the email typed left to
  right in Markazi's Latin. Validation on blur; the error clears when fixed; a failed submit moves focus to the first
  wrong field, whose border turns red.
- **Sign in:** 48 px, full width, the berry pen's fill. Busy: spinner, «جارٍ تسجيل الدخول…», `aria-busy`.
- **«إنشاء حساب»:** a drawn box, ink on paper, on the facing page.
- **Errors in red pen:** no box. A red «!» in the margin beside it, the title written in the hand in red with a wavy
  red underline («تعذّر تسجيل الدخول»), the explanation in print below. One-line errors (too many attempts, no
  connection) are written in the hand in red. `role="alert"`; focus moves to it.
- **Notes in berry pen:** the session note is print with a berry line down its start side.
- **Small links** have a hit area of 44 px or more drawn outside the text, so nothing moves.

## Motion

None. The notebook is still; only the spinner turns (slower under reduced motion).

## Accessibility

Print 8.5 : 1 or more; berry words 5.7 : 1; red pen 5.9 : 1; controls drawn in full ink. Targets ≥ 44 px. The ruling
is 1.2 : 1 against the paper (1.15 : 1 at night), texture, never information. Keyboard order, checked by tabbing in
Chrome: logo, language, theme, way back, Google, email, forgotten password, password, show, Sign in, Create account,
Contact us, privacy. The sketch is `aria-hidden`; the margin note is real text, read before the paragraph it labels.

## What it keeps from the product's design system, and what it changes

**Keeps:** the ground `#F7FBFF` and the ink `#1A1F25`; the spark ramp (s-500, s-600, s-400); the status reds (r-600,
r-400); Western digits; logical properties; the logo at 40 px, uncoloured, never mirrored; submit stays enabled, focus to
the first invalid field; underlined links in sentences.

**Changes, and why:**
- *The spark as words and on an action.* The system never lets the spark be text or an action. Here it is the pen: a
  few handwritten words in s-600 (5.7 : 1) and the sign-in button. It is the direction's whole idea.
- *Errors without the destructive-subtle box.* The red is the system's, but the error is written in red pen on the
  page rather than set in a pink panel: on paper, that is what an error looks like.
- *Three new typefaces.* Aref Ruqaa, Kalam (English only) and Markazi Text replace IBM Plex Sans Arabic, because the
  voice of this direction is handwriting over print. Plex would turn it back into a form on graph paper.
- *Sizes.* Print at 21 px rather than 15 px (Markazi's small eye), the title at 88 px rather than 30 px.
- *Shape.* 3 px corners instead of 6–8 px, and inked 1.5 px borders instead of n-500 hairlines: drawn, not rounded.
- *Muted text.* n-700 rather than the system's n-600, for a thin serif on a ruled ground.
